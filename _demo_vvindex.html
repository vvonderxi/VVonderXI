What the number cannot see

Two limits that no amount of data collection fixes, because they are about what football records, not about what we gathered.

Halfof the job
The game records what a defender does far more faithfully than what he prevents. The tackle is counted. The run that made the tackle unnecessary is not, and neither is the pass a striker never attempted because the space was gone.

So the best defenders score lower here than their reputation, and we are not going to fix that by inventing a number. Van Dijk sits in the low seventies, and we publish that with the reason rather than quietly moving him. A score that agrees with every reputation is not measuring reputation, it is copying it.
Topof the ladder
Positions are verified by hand near the top of the ladder and get coarser below it. A high score is a claim we have checked. A low one is an estimate.

What the number chose

Not a limit. A decision, and one that shapes every score on the platform.

Attackthe lean
The scale tilts toward attacking output, because that is where the evidence is , goals and assists are recorded everywhere and back to 2010, and defending is not. We say so rather than claiming a balance the record cannot support.

Where the ground still moves

A score is a standing, not a mark out of ten, so it answers to every season measured beside it.

Seasons that changed band the last time the record grew. We recovered 780 missing seasons across all nine leagues, and exactly one card crossed a line , nothing about that player changed, only the company he was being measured against.

We changed how defenders are scored. 10,770 seasons moved. Not one crossed a band, and we are telling you anyway.

The defensive signal was two measures blended into one number. It is one measure now, and the biggest single mover was Azpilicueta's 2018, from 70 to 72.
