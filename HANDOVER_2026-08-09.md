## 2. POSITION WORK , the thread that dominated this session

Every card sits in one of **8 position buckets**: GK, FB, CB, CDM, CM, CAM, Winger, ST. A systematic upstream fault was dumping wingers into **CAM**, so the bucket was polluted.

**140 cards reclassified, in seven write batches, all rt-verified before and after:**

| batch | cards | source |
|---|---|---|
| Tier A | 24 | Transfermarkt, hand-verified |
| pass 2 | 31 | two independent research passes agreeing |
| tail 01 | 35 | Fable research, batch 01 |
| tail 02-05 | 40 | Fable research, batches 02-05 |
| Coman | 7 | Transfermarkt, rule-qualified (see below) |
| Nico Williams | 2 | manual override, source `knowledge` |

CAM pool went **1,870 → 1,732**. Across all 140 there was **ONE public band crossing**: Souček 20/21, rt 79 → 82.

**THE RULE THAT CLEARED COMAN, and it is narrower than it looks.** A held row can be cleared without per-season research ONLY when the player's Transfermarkt **main position AND every listed other position are wide**. Coman is main Left Winger, other Right Winger, no central or striker role anywhere. **The broader "his other seasons were Winger, so this one is too" heuristic was TESTED AND FAILED**: of 11 players checked, 10 had a wide main position, but Pépé's other positions are Centre-Forward and Second Striker (so a held season of his might be ST), and Coutinho is main **Attacking Midfield** with wide secondaries. Bulk-clearing on same-player would have written plausible, untraceable errors. **25 rows for eight more players are still held** , Transfermarkt went into maintenance mid-pass, and no substitute source was used.

**THE RULE THAT EXPLAINS IT, and the next batch will otherwise be surprised by it: CDM is the only destination pool where the defensive signal is load-bearing.** All four cards that moved rt at all were `CAM -> CDM`. Every `CAM -> Winger` and `CAM -> CM` row moved exactly zero, because for attackers the bounded `def_core` loses to the attacking path in the best-of. **Expect rt movement only on CDM transitions, and expect it upward.**

### Batches 06-21 were deliberately NOT run

The tail was split into 21 batches ordered by rt descending. Only 01-05 ran. **This is a decision backed by measurement, not an oversight.**

The research reports a confidence level per row, and only `high` rows are written. That rate collapsed as the players got less prominent:

```
batch 01  avg rt 78.1   65% high      batch 04  avg rt 60.3   3% high
batch 02  avg rt 64.0   15% high      batch 05  avg rt 56.6   3% high
batch 03  avg rt 64.4   13% high
```

**At 3%, running the remaining ~1,150 cards would put ~97% of them straight into a hold file.** Below roughly rt56 the players are non-famous and in smaller leagues, and reliable per-season role information genuinely runs out. Either accept the tail stays CAM-labelled and disclose it, or find a source that actually covers small-league players. **Do not just run it because it is there.**

**A falling curve is the healthy signal. A FLAT curve is a WARNING** , it means the research stopped saying UNSURE and started guessing with a confident label. The writer warns automatically on two consecutive non-falling batches.

**If the tail is ever revisited:** the prompt is `cam_tail_batches/PROMPT.md` (tracked, load-bearing , it carries the UNSURE emphasis and the identity contract). Batches 01-05 are tracked; 06-21 are gitignored and regenerate with `NODE_PATH=./node_modules node scripts/enrichment/make_cam_tail_batches.js`.

## 3. TAG WORK , done, verified, but the numbers will drift

Cards carry descriptive tags (Goal Machine, Playmaker, The Wall...). The thresholds were **percentile-only with no absolute floor**, so small raw totals over small minutes cleared elite per-90 rates. Live symptoms: a 6-goal season held "Goal Machine"; a 1-assist season held "Playmaker".

**Fixed and shipped in `c4ad9e2`.** Raw floors added (Goal Machine ≥12 goals, Marksman ≥8, Playmaker ≥3 assists), an undocumented `×0.92` softening removed so p90 means p90, and every per-90 tag gated at ≥900 minutes. A missing stat is **exempt** from a raw floor, because **NR is not zero**.

Verified two ways: a parity harness proved the applied code matches the approved simulation on 0 of 57,234 rows differing, and the result was then **checked on the actual rendered cards**, not just in a table.

**Cards carrying at least one tag: 37.2% → 26.0%.**

**THE PERCENTAGES ARE PROVISIONAL.** Tag thresholds are keyed to the coarse FWD/MID/DEF family, so every position reclassification moves cards between threshold tables and changes which bar a card is measured against. **Re-run the simulation once the position work settles.** Do not quote the current figures as final.

## 5. RULES YOU MUST NOT REDISCOVER THE HARD WAY

- **Provenance does not generalise across fields.** For **position**, research (Transfermarkt, Sofascore) beats API-Football, because API-Football does not state a position , it *infers* one from a lineup grid cell, which is the root cause of the whole CAM problem. For **assists**, the opposite holds: FBref is authoritative and research is not. **Do not carry one field's source hierarchy over to another.**
- **Write on `api_player_id`, never on display name.** Names are not unique here and the collisions are live and position-changing: `J. Rodríguez` is three different players, `João Mário` two, `Nenê` two, `L. Pellegrini` two, and in every case the collision partner plays a **different position**. A name-keyed write puts a wrong position on a wrong player and looks completely plausible in the diff. The writers enforce this in code; do not bypass it.
- **When a rendered page and a measurement disagree, the render wins. Go and look.** Theme toggling animates colour, so `getComputedStyle` mid-transition returns interpolated garbage. That produced a fabricated bug report and a commit with a false number in it. Use `withTheme()` in `_audit.js` for anything theme-dependent.
- **Before overriding a style, confirm the selector actually matches the element.** Four consecutive failed fixes went to one element because every attempt targeted a selector that matched nothing.
- **A missing signal is not evidence.** An empty result can mean "broken" or "the harness cannot see this". Establish a positive control before declaring anything broken.
- **`player_card_mv` is a materialized view with a frozen column list.** Adding a column to the underlying view surfaces it nowhere; it needs a DROP + CREATE of the matview plus its 8 indexes. Always `REFRESH MATERIALIZED VIEW player_card_mv` after any data or view change.
- **No em-dashes or en-dashes anywhere.** Spaced commas. `NR` for missing data, never 0.
