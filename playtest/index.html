      How to play

      Get all seven of your seasons into your Legacy before your rival does. Every turn is a match moment: whoever has possession picks it, after the other side strikes one of three off. You both play a season face-down. Reveal. Bigger number wins the moment. Win it, and that season walks into your Legacy. Win it big, and it takes a teammate with it.

      Two outcomes, four names. A win sends your season into your Legacy. A big win sends a teammate of your choice in with him (an Assist). The loser's season goes back to their squad. Substitutions: three per match, at most one per turn, used while you strike off or pick a moment: change players (swap any of your seasons for random ones from the Bench) or a Tactical Switch (three new moments, only when you attack). Captain mode: you secretly name one season Captain at kick-off; it can only go into your Legacy last. Big Game is played on real VV Score points and uses the Compare page's own margins.
