**THREE THINGS THAT MUST NOT BE RE-DERIVED:**
- **Qualifying and play-off rounds are two-legged KNOCKOUTS.** Classified on tie structure, not
  on the word in the label. A consumer reading the stage name and treating qualifying as
  "not knockout" applies the wrong extra-time rule , a scores defect in labelling clothes.
- **The neutral-venue flag is a curated LIST, not a measurement.** High precision, unknown
  recall. The venue-versus-home-club check was REJECTED: ground shares and temporary stadiums
  produce false neutrals, and a false neutral on a real home tie corrupts home advantage,
  which is the thing BAM models. Not used as a cross-reference either , a second signal that
  disagreed would need adjudicating per fixture and nobody would know which was right.
- **Page size is per-endpoint, not per-API.** A guard verified on one endpoint does not
  transfer. The tell is a round number where the domain does not produce one , a league season
  is 182 or 306 or 552, never exactly 100. Same shape as the PostgREST 1000-row cap in SS C.
