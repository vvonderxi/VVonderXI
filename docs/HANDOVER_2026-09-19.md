## THE FAILURE SHAPES THIS DAY PRODUCED , THE REUSABLE PART

**1. A SELECTOR THAT MATCHES NOTHING LOOKS EXACTLY LIKE A RULE NEVER WRITTEN. THREE TODAY.**
`.vquote:first-of-type` matched on element type, not class, and was inert. The Fraunces rules
lost on specificity to later rules and rendered nothing. The demo's club-line placement matched
`&middot;` against a serialised literal `·` and drew no glyph. **None errored.** The guard is to
make the no-match case LOUD: assert the computed value, or throw when the anchor is missing.

**2. A DEMO MUST RENDER IN THE FONTS THE TARGET SURFACE SHIPS.** A verdict demo set in Fraunces
for a page that loaded no serif would have added a fifth webfont family by the back door, and
its "ships today" column was misdrawn too. Read the target's `family=` list first. A new face is
its own decision with its own cost, which is how Fraunces was eventually taken, priced at 66 KB.

**3. A BOUND IS NOT A READING.** The CSS walker scores a gradient's WORST stop. On the card-face
chips that bound accepted three exceptions that do not exist (4.37 bound, 4.82 and 5.50 read);
on the compare CTA it said 2.16 where the glyphs sit at 2.77. Always in the same direction,
always looking like a finding.

**4. A CONTROL THAT NEVER FIRES IS INDISTINGUISHABLE FROM ONE THAT DOES NOT WORK.** Every
verification today carried a control that had to change: `.pspot` 3.89 for the harness, a click
on the photo that must flip before a click on the mark proved zero flips, a swipe that must page
before a tap proved no page. Twice a first measurement was void: an impact check compared OLD
with OLD because the snapshot was not exported, and a real click was aimed in the wrong
coordinate frame and landed on the card face. **The control is what told each apart.**

**5. THE COUNT OF A LIST IS A CLAIM THAT NEEDS A DERIVATION, NOT A MEMORY.** The punchlist read
15 of 25 when it held 35 rows; the first fix said 20 of 34. The BAM club-name finding was quoted
as "94 of 108" and is 93 of 94. **The awk in PUNCHLIST's header derives the count; the README
