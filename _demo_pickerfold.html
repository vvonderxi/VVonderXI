Picker fold at 390 x 667

Measured in a single iframe on the live compare.html, viewport asserted from inside (innerWidth 390, innerHeight 667, both phone queries matching) before any reading was taken. Not modelled.
The premise was wrong in two ways, and the second one matters. The panel does not open below the fold , it is position:fixed and bottom-anchored at y=667, growing upward. And in its default state the picker is fine: results render 347px tall and fully visible. The defect appears only when the filters are opened, which costs 255px of results.

The rail's content is 1,310px across 8 groups and 58 chips, but it is already internally bounded , it contributes about 321px to the panel's flow, not 1,310. That is why capping it at 200px buys only 24px and capping at 140px buys 84px. An earlier note assumed the rail pushes results down by its full height. It does not.

Recommendation

B, capped at 140px, and nothing else. It is one declaration in the phone media query, it introduces no mode and no new control, and it recovers 84 of the 255px the filters cost. C is rejected , it adds a mode and an Apply step to fix a state the reader is only in briefly, and it hides the results completely while they choose.

And the honest bound: B does not fully fix it. Results go from 92px to 176px of 347. The remaining cost is the panel being bottom-anchored to a 667px viewport with 106px of chrome above the rail. Getting beyond 176 means changing the panel's anchoring, which is a rework of the flow and is not what was asked for.
Placement rule, from CLAUDE.md SS C: a media query adds no specificity. The block goes last in the sheet, and the fix is verified by reading the computed value, never by confirming the query matched. That has bitten three times in one session before.
