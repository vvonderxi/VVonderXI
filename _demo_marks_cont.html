Continental trophies , judged as a set

Two new marks against the seven that exist. The green cells are the new ones.
THE SET'S DESIGN RULE, WHICH BOTH NEW MARKS OBEY: team honours stand footed and vertical; individual awards are unfooted objects. That is what tells a reader whether a trophy was won by a squad or by a person, before they read the label.

AND THE COLLISION THAT MATTERS IS BETWEEN SILHOUETTES AT 16px, the size a pill ships at. The three existing team marks occupy three shapes , the World Cup a globe and ring, the UCL its big side ears, the League a wide bowl on a boxed plinth. The two new ones take the shapes that family leaves free: the Euro a tall slim body on a small foot, body-dominant; the Copa a small bowl over a triangular plinth, base-dominant and the inverse of it. The discriminator is the body-to-base ratio, which survives rasterisation when handle detail does not.
