C , The ball stays a ball. The data goes round it.

A football's panels are FIVE-fold symmetric and our data is NINE-fold, so any attempt to make the wedges BE the panels gives up one job or the other , A is a pie wearing a pentagon, B is a pie. This keeps a recognisable ball and puts the nine leagues in a ring around it, where 36° to 44.5° is legible as "near-equal" rather than pretending to be a panel layout.
