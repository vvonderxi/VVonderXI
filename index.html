📈 SSP Rating Scale — What Each Grade Means

The SSP Rating is the scout's interpretation of the VV Score. A rating is not a measure of talent — it is a measure of seasonal impact relative to role, position, league context and career stage.

💫 S-Tier (22+/25)
A season of genuine historical significance. When the VV Engine returns an S-Tier grade, it means the output is exceptional not merely in absolute terms, but after full contextual adjustment — role, position, league difficulty, career stage. Fewer than one in a hundred seasons across the entire database reach this threshold. These are not celebrated campaigns. They are reference points. The seasons against which everything else is measured.
🌍 Generational (19–22)
Exceptional output that transcends the immediate — a campaign that rewrites what is considered possible for that role. The VV Engine awards this grade only when adjusted impact clearly separates a player from all but a handful of contemporaries. These are the seasons that outlive the trophy cabinets they belong to. Scouts do not describe them. They quote them.
🏆 Era Defining (16.5–19)
The highest sustained standard at which professional football is consistently played. A player carrying this grade has not merely performed well — they have shaped how their position is understood. Their output influences transfer valuations, tactical decisions and the benchmarks against which future players are measured. A genuinely rare classification that demands sustained excellence across every dimension.
🌟 World Class (14–16.5)
Among the finest performers in the world for their position in this window. Not simply productive — positionally impactful, technically refined, and consistently influential at the highest level of competition. These players command attention in scouting meetings, in transfer negotiations and on the pitch. Other managers do not simply try to beat them. They plan specifically to stop them.
👑 Elite (11.5–14)
Firmly within the conversation for the most impactful performers in their league. Elite is not a participation grade — it requires sustained quality across output, influence and availability. A player at this level changes matches, justifies their squad position and raises the ceiling of the teams they play in. They are the players clubs fight to keep and rivals scheme to acquire.
⭐ Excellent (9–11.5)
High-level, consistent professional impact across the season. A player graded Excellent has contributed meaningfully above the baseline expected of their role. They are reliable, they are impactful and they perform at a standard most professional footballers across all levels never reach. Their contribution is felt most clearly in their absence.
🟢 Very Good (6.5–9)
Clearly above the professional average for their position and league. A player returning a Very Good SSP grade has delivered on what was asked of them — and then some. They may not command the headline, but in any objective squad assessment they improve the team they are part of. Dependable, purposeful, and operating above the median at their level.
🔵 Good (4–6.5)
A solid professional contribution that meets the functional requirements of the role. Good is not a consolation grade — squads are built on players who deliver this level of consistency. The output is present, the contribution is real, the influence is positive. Not every campaign needs to be historic. Good is the standard most professionals aspire to reach and many never do.
🟠 Okay (2–4)
Below the threshold for meaningful impact at the competition level in question. The numbers are present but insufficient to support a strong positive assessment. Context matters enormously here — an Okay season from a teenager making their first starts reads differently to the same grade from an established international in their prime years. The grade reflects impact, not effort.
🔴 Poor (0–2)
The output does not support the case being made for this player at this level. Across the five dimensions — output, influence, consistency, league context and positional expectation — the aggregate falls below the acceptable floor. This assessment is positional and contextual, not personal. Every player experiences these seasons. What a career is built on is the response.

💎 Role Rarity — The Hidden Multiplier

When a defender or defensive midfielder produces attacking output that breaks their positional expectation, the VV Engine awards a Role Rarity bonus of up to +5 points. This can flip a comparison.

Example: Trent Alexander-Arnold
1G 10A as a Right Back = Era Defining output for the role. The same numbers from a winger would be Very Good. The Role Rarity bonus reflects the structural rarity of a defender producing at this creative level.
Example: Virgil van Dijk peak
A CB with an rt of 92 and 3G 2A earns a Rarity bonus on top of their Influence score. Pure defensive excellence combined with any attacking contribution from a CB is genuinely rare.

🎬 What is Baller Bio?

Baller Bio is the VVonderXI player identity card. It uses the same VV Engine as the comparison system, but instead of producing a verdict, it generates a scout-style football profile — who the player is, what they do, and what their numbers actually mean.

Use the Baller Bio tab in the Compare section to generate analyses. Select two players, switch to Baller Bio mode, and run the analysis to receive a football identity overview for each player.

📋 How to Read a Baller Bio

Player Profile
Who the player is as a footballer. Their style, their strengths, the tactical role they fill. Written in the VVonderXI voice — analytical, precise, not generic.
Career Stage
Where they sit in their arc — Wonderkid, Prospect, Rising Star, Emerging Elite, Prime, Veteran, Legend. Age, output and trajectory all inform this.
Trajectory
The 3-season momentum read. Is the player improving, peaking, stable or declining? The direction of travel often matters more than the current level.
Why It Matters in Comparisons
Baller Bio context changes how a comparison is read. A player at their Peak means something different from a player at Emergence with the same numbers.

League Strength Weighting

VVonderXI applies UEFA coefficient-based difficulty weights. Identical output in different leagues carries different real value.

Hidden Scout Weighting

Every comparison uses a hidden scoring system. The AI and analysis engine weight these factors:

Season Performance (goals, assists, rating)30%
League Strength (UEFA coefficient factor)20%
Age Context (career stage premium/discount)20%
Position Context (benchmarks per role)15%
Trajectory (momentum across seasons)10%
Availability / Minutes5%

Position Benchmarks

ST / CF — Striker
Elite: 22+ goals. Strong: 15+. Functional: 10+. Below: <10. Goals are the primary metric.
LW / RW — Winger
Elite: 25+ G+A. Strong: 18+. Functional: 12+. Balance of goals and assists valued equally.
CAM — Attacking Mid
Elite: 20+ G+A. Strong: 14+. Assists weighted slightly above goals for this role.
CM — Central Mid
Elite: 15+ G+A. Strong: 10+. Both goal threat and creativity expected from modern CMs.
CDM / CB / LB / RB
G+A is a bonus, not the primary metric. Rating and defensive contribution define this role.
GK — Goalkeeper
G+A is near-irrelevant. Rating is the sole meaningful output metric.
