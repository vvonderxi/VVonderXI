Add to home screen , the three states, and the Android fork

The strip is the one that ships on the home page today, copied here so you judge the real thing. What is new is the iOS step list with the share glyph drawn, and the second Android variant. It sits at the bottom of the home page on phones only, after the second visit, and never returns once dismissed.

The fork is a decision, not a detail: Chrome fires its native prompt only for a site with a service worker that has a real fetch handler. Without one, the browser can still install from its own menu, but our Add button has nothing to call.
