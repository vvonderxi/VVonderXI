Page transitions , three options, real navigations

Pick a mode, then press Go to the other page. Each press is a genuine document navigation to this same file with a different ?p=, so what you see is what the option would actually do between two pages of the platform.

What this demo cannot show. How any of it feels on a mid-range phone over a real network. On localhost with a warm cache the load is a few milliseconds, so the transition IS the experience; on a cold connection the page is slower than every curve here and the transition is a smaller part of it. Judge the shape here, the feel on a device.
