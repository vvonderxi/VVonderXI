Item 26 , what the shield shows when the number may be the wrong club's

Real cards, real stored rows, rendered by the shipped buildCard with card.html's own stylesheet pulled in at load, in the fonts that page ships. Only the shield number is altered, and only where a caption says so. Nothing here is built.

Two questions, and the first changes the second.
1. What does a blank shield mean now? On 09-12 it meant "deliberately left blank" and 20,063 cards carried one. The squad-number backfill has since filled most of them: 12,298 blank today, 12,135 of them before 2016. So blank now reads as "we did not find it", not as a choice.
2. What does a flagged number show? If blank means "not found", putting a flagged card into that state says something false: we did find a number, we just cannot vouch for which club it belongs to. That is the argument for a third state (C or D) over B.
Read this before choosing: the flag is not yet precise enough to render. The detector (pos_row_appearances > appearances) returns 841 cards, but a proxy for "really moved" (the player is at a different club the season before or after) runs at 49% on the 364 cards with a gap of 1-2, against a 34% baseline for cards nobody flagged. On gaps of 3 and up it is 70-75%. So most small-gap flags are not transfers: Tadić at Ajax 18/19 (gap 1) never moved, and neither did Undav at Union 21/22 (gap 14, the Belgian play-offs). Whatever treatment you pick, the population it applies to is a second decision, and the demo shows a false positive so you can see what it costs.
