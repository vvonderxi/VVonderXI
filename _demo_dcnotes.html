Data Confidence · #s-conf

Shirt numbers. Every number on a card records where it came from, and there are three answers.

Sourced. Read off that club's own squad and appearance tables for that season, and cross-checked against each other where a page carries more than one. No arrows.

One club that season. He played for a single club, so there is no question whose number it is. No arrows.

Inferred. He moved mid-season, and this is the number he wore most often across the whole season, which may belong to either club. These are the cards that carry the arrows.

Sourced is not the same as confirmed. These numbers come from squad tables written by volunteers, and we say where a number came from rather than that it is right.
