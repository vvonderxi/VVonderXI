Under the Lights

The glow is #cardA::before / #cardB::before , a pink radial ellipse behind each card at blur(36px), alpha 0.30 dark and 0.20 light. Everything below runs on the real compare page in the frame to the right, not a mock, by overriding that one rule.

"Frame time" is the wrong unit here and saying so is part of the answer. This is a STATIC background: it paints on load, on resize, and on a theme change, and never again. Nothing animates it, so it costs nothing per frame while a visitor reads. The cost that is real is the one-off blur, and blur scales with area x radius, which is arithmetic rather than a timing run , SEC C records that this harness cannot measure frames at all.
