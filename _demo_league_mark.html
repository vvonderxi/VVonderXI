Redrawing the League Title mark

It ships on 2,872 cards, the most common honour in the record, so this is the most visible mark on the platform. Today it is a bowl on a stem over a plinth, and the World Cup mark is a ring on a stem over a base: two trophies on stems, which is why they converge at small sizes. Each candidate below leaves the stem behind.

Every candidate is scored against all eleven marks it can appear beside , the other eight honours plus Generational and Iconic, which sit in the same row on a card face , not just against the World Cup. Same method as the existing harness, and within this run only, since absolute values drift by method.
