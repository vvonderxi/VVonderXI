Why not one sentence for both

A single “not scored, under 300 minutes” would be true of both and would tell the reader of B nothing about the thing they noticed , that there used to be a 53 there. And telling the reader of A that a score was “split away” would be false: their card is new. B names the real minutes and says “used to cover two clubs” rather than “used to score 53” , the platform explains why there is no number, it does not publish the number it has just withdrawn.
