The wash isn't missing from the verdict. It was never defined for dark at all.

--vvem-wash exists only under body.light, and the rule that applies it is also gated on body.light. In dark, emphasis is gold ink and nothing else, on every prose surface, on both pages.
And there are two grounds, not one , read off the live page, not the stylesheet. The verdict quote sits on solid rgb(42,36,34). The story prose sits on the Under-the-Lights green gradient, rgb(28,96,56) → rgb(15,58,34). One wash value cannot serve both. There is already precedent for splitting: --vvem-ink carries two values, and its own comment says that is "because the ground moves, not because the theme does".

Measured, not judged by eye

The second column is the one that decides it. A wash that clears 4.5 against the ink but sits 1.05 from the ground around it is invisible , it reads as no wash at all, which is where we are now. It has to separate from BOTH.
Pick one per ground, or say the wash is wrong for dark and the answer is a heavier ink. My reading: A on the charcoal and D on the green , both lift enough to read as a marked phrase without becoming a highlighter, and both keep the gold comfortably over the bar.
