A CONSTRAINT THAT COMES WITH A2, AND IT IS NOT OPTIONAL

A caption for a THREE-card season may not name three clubs in sequence. Four player-seasons carry three cards , Dragus 2025/26, Depaoli 2020/21, Posch 2024/25 and Unai Nunez 2025/26 , and orderSeasonRows consults a transfer row only when the group holds exactly two, so all four take the appearances fallback. It is measurably wrong on at least two: Depaoli is exactly reversed against the export. So the caption generator must do what partialSeasonNote already does for the same case , the undated form, clubs listed ALPHABETICALLY, no "then" , until the sequence work in docs/CROSS_LEAGUE_ORDER_SCOPE.md section 5 ships. The axis labels are already handled: renderTrajectory labels a year holding exactly two cards and leaves three unlabelled.

My recommendation: A2

One mechanism beats two. A1 names clubs in two different places depending on how many seasons a player has, and the reader cannot see the reason , the fit is a measurement they never get to make. A2 puts the clubs in one place on every card, and it is the only option that can HEDGE: where we have no dated transfer the caption can say “and” instead of “then”, which an axis label cannot do at all. It also removes shipped machinery rather than adding it , the fit pass, the hidden-until-measured state and the ResizeObserver all go, and the chart returns to a pure string.

What A2 costs, stated plainly: on a short card you lose the direct bar-to-club mapping that you approved. The caption names them in order and the bars are drawn in that order, so the mapping survives , but only if the order is right, which is exactly what docs/CROSS_LEAGUE_ORDER_SCOPE.md is for. A2 should not ship before that fix does.

B is the one I would not take. It charges every split card 40 per cent of its height to serve the dense minority, and it is the only option that makes the short case worse than it is today.
