VV search , current vs proposed

Demo only. Hits the live matview with the anon key. Does not touch rankings.html or compare.html.
Current , name_norm + team_norm, rt floor 50, cap 300
Proposed , + display name, match-count specificity, relevance rank
Proposed rule. Count the text matches with no rt floor. ≤ 300 → SPECIFIC: return them all, no floor, ranked by name-match quality. > 300 → BROAD: rt floor + rt order + limit 300, i.e. today's browse behaviour unchanged. The ceiling is both the threshold and the cap, so "no cap" never means unbounded.

What differs. The proposed side adds player_name (the display name) as a third match column, drops the rt floor for specific queries, and orders by relevance instead of rt. Rows the current side cannot reach at all are the ones that were invisible to search.
