The verdict

The Index separates them by four points, and the recorded figures explain why. Messi's season was a personal act of will inside a broken club, authenticated by a Ballon d'Or and a Golden Boot, and it carries the full weight of everything the modern data can measure. Ronaldo's 2014/15 output, even sketched only in goals and assists, describes something the system rarely sees, a winger at 29 producing at the kind of volume that shifts the ceiling rather than meeting it.

This is a real but contested edge. Messi's season deserves to stand on its own terms for what it cost him to produce it. Ronaldo's takes the argument on the numbers that matter most.

Messi's card is richer in evidence, but Ronaldo's is richer in result.
