What to judge

1. Does it hold? Worst frame under about 20ms is clean. A run of 30ms+ frames is what reads as jank, and on the platform's hero element a janky animation is worse than a hard cut. If it will not hold, say so and we take the crossfade , it moves the least and is the cheapest thing on this page.

2. Do the two axes read as ONE grammar? Vertical and horizontal use the SAME duration and the SAME easing, cubic-bezier(.5,0,.5,1) at 800ms, lifted from the season flip that already ships. Only the axis differs. If they feel like two different languages, that is the thing to report.

3. Reduced motion. The banner above says what your device is asking for. The card already honours it elsewhere, so a reader who asks for less gets the hard cut and no movement at all , switch it on in your OS settings and the buttons should stop animating.

The premise was inverted, and it is worth keeping

Horizontal was named as the model to follow and it turned out to be the deficit. Season switching already calls vvCardFlip , 800ms, a 3D flip on desktop and a scale-swap through the VV coin on mobile. Player switching had no transition at all: scroll to top, re-render, hard cut.

The instinct , copy the thing that feels finished , is usually right. It was wrong here because the thing that felt finished was the one nobody had built.
