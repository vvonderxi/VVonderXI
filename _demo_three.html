Three things to look at

The season stepper, the Playbook fold, and the PDF mark. Every number here was measured, not estimated.

1 , The stepper "bounce" is geometry, not easing

There is no curve to choose. The card changes size at the two moments the transition starts and ends, which is outside the animated interval, so no easing can reach it.
At rest a card is width:var(--cw) with max-width:92%. The flip wraps it in an element carrying max-width:none , on both the desktop and mobile paths , which removes the clamp. The card jumps wider, the animation runs, and the wrapper is torn down, so it snaps back.

It is surface-dependent, and that is the part I did not expect. max-width:92% is 92% of the parent, not of --cw. On compare's two-up phone layout the slot is narrow enough that the clamp never binds, so there is no jump at all. You are seeing this on the card page, and you would not see it on compare. The jump is 26px on a 300px card.
The fix: keep the resting clamp inside the flip and size the back face to match it , max-width:92% on the two flip-front rules, and width:calc(var(--cw)*0.92) on the back face and the mobile coin cover. Measured after: 0.00px change on both axes, on desktop and at 390, with front and back matching. It is a no-op where the clamp does not bind, so compare is unaffected.

2 , "Read the full story" , the label and the pill

Two things, and the second is the stronger argument.
The label overpromises: the fold opens 108 words, six sentences.
And it breaks the page's own convention: "See more ›" is used 29 times on the Playbook for exactly this fold. "Read the full story ›" is used twice , here and in one other place , for less content than most of the 29.

Current , the closed state is the problem

A white box styled to hold content, containing only its own toggle. That is the awkwardness , the pill is sized for prose that is not there yet.

A , same fold, the page's own label

One word changed. Consistent with 29 other folds, and no longer promising an essay.

B , no box until it opens

The container goes transparent while closed, so the link sits in the flow and the box appears only with the prose. Removes the floating pill entirely.

C , do not fold it at all

Five numbers, but never only numbers. They are the shape of a player , the fingerprint no two careers share. One man lives for the goal; another for the pass that breathes it into being…
108 words is about 25 seconds. A fold costs a tap and hides the best writing on the page. This is the one I would pick , the fold exists to protect the reader from length, and there is not enough length to protect them from.

3 , The PDF has no mark at all

Not misplaced , absent. docs/pdf/vv-index.html contains zero images and zero SVG.
The home-page mark is two PNGs, dark and light, swapped by theme. The PDF prints on white, so it takes the light one. Two things have to be right and neither shows on screen: the path must resolve when the file is opened from the site root, and print-color-adjust must keep it from being dropped. Verified in print preview, not on screen , that is where a background image silently disappears.
