What this is

VVonderXI is a football statistics site. It publishes ratings for historical player seasons. It is free to use.

What it is not

It is not a betting service, a tipping service, a prediction service or a financial product. Nothing here is advice of any kind. The scores describe seasons that have already happened. They are not forecasts and must not be treated as a basis for wagering or for any other decision with money attached.

Accuracy

Scores are generated from third party match data and from our own model. We work to keep both correct and we will fix errors when they are found, but we do not warrant that every figure is free of mistakes. If you think a score is wrong, tell us.

The model

The VV Index and the VV Score are ours. You are welcome to reference a score and link back to it. You may not scrape the site, republish the dataset, or present the scores as your own.
